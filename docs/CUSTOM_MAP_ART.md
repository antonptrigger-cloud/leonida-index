# Custom map art (base map "art")

The default base map on `/map` is the owner's own AI-generated illustration of Leonida, cleaned, upscaled and tiled.
It is **illustrative, not the in-game geography**. The old placeholder map stays available as a fallback (`/map?base=legacy`,
or the "Base map" selector in the filters) and Rockstar's official map can replace either one later.

## What the base is made of

| Piece | Source |
|---|---|
| Whole map | `assets/map/art/full-orig.jpg` (1200x1648) |
| Vice City detail | `assets/map/art/vc-orig.jpg` (1840x1072), aligned onto the whole map (SIFT + RANSAC similarity, 527 inliers) and blended in with a feathered, colour-matched mask |
| Text, scale bar, grid numbers, watermarks | removed from both images with LaMa inpainting (tight boxes), before upscaling |
| Grid hairlines, fake-text clusters, worst blobs | removed by `scripts/art/clean_artifacts.py` (see "Art cleanup" below) |
| Resolution | Real-ESRGAN `realesr-general-x4v3` x4 on both, composited at **7200 x 9888** (6x the whole-map pixels; the VC detail is native or better there) |
| Tiles | 256px webp pyramid, z0 to z6 (z6 = 1 source px per screen px), 1540 tiles, about 4 MB, `public/map/art/`. The map stops at **z6.5** (a soft cap, see "Honest zoom" below) |

Pipeline and scripts: `scripts/art/README.md`. Output is committed, so nothing here runs on deploy.

### Art cleanup (`scripts/art/clean_artifacts.py`)
Runs after `clean_text.py` and before the upscale. It removes what was still visible at z6 once the baked text was gone:

1. **Grid hairlines and node glyphs.** The source art has a thin survey grid (about 1px dark/blue lines every ~110px, a heavier
   cross at x=889 / y=894 of the full map) with a small ring glyph at each crossing. The lines are removed by *subtracting a measured
   per-channel line profile in 32px blocks* (`remove_lines`), not by inpainting, so roads and rivers that cross a line keep their
   detail (a Telea inpaint smeared them). The ring glyphs on water get a tiny Telea inpaint.
2. **Garbled fake-text clusters.** Found automatically (`water_specks`, bright high-frequency specks on open water) plus a
   hand-checked box list (`FULL_TEXT_BOXES`, `VC_TEXT_BOXES`): the Vice City port water, leftovers under the Keys main island, red
   glyphs east of the Keys, specks in the west ocean and the remains of a watermark. All removed with LaMa.
3. **The worst AI blobs.** Two grey/white discs that read as broken art at z6: the Leonida Keys main island (an invented two-runway
   "X" with tan taxiways) and the Port Gellhorn disc (a smudged brown rectangle). `paint_land` keeps the blob's outline (so the
   coastline stays) and fills it with plain land colour sampled from the surrounding land plus soft texture and a sandy rim.
   It does **not** invent streets or buildings. They are deliberately plain: better an honest empty island than a fake airport.

Then `upscale.py`, `align.py` and `composite.py <full-x4> <vc-x4>` (the composite script now takes the two upscales as arguments)
and `npm run map:tiles` as before. Known leftover: a very faint light strip along the heavy x=889 line outside the Vice City
detail area. Before/after crops are in the PR description.

### Labels, pin names and the base-map chip
* **Labels** (`src/lib/map/labels.json`, generated by `scripts/art/build_labels.py`): 6 region names plus 9 district/place/water
  names, **all taken from official Rockstar pages** (each entry stores the page and the wording in `sourceUrl` / `sourceQuote`).
  They are stored in pin space and run through the active base's transform, like pins, so they work on both bases. Regions become
  big letter-spaced text once the region chips fade out; the rest appear by zoom. Every position is approximate (`approximate: true`,
  with the reason in `placement`). Rockstar publishes no coordinates, so districts without a pin (Stockyard, Southside) are placed
  by direction only, using the relative layout of GTADB's data (credited in `docs/DATA_CREDITS.md`). A name Rockstar uses but nobody
  can place (Gambit Bay) gets no label.
* **Pin names** show from z6 (native resolution) beside their marker, with collision thinning: pin markers and clusters claim space
  first, then region names, then labels by priority (region, water, district, place), then pin names (selected, then official). Whatever
  does not fit is dropped and comes back when zooming in. The layout is computed in projected pixels and only changes on zoom.
* **Honest zoom.** The art's real detail ends around z4 (about z5 inside Vice City). z6 is one source pixel per screen pixel, so
  anything beyond is the same pixels enlarged. The art base now caps at **z6.5** (`ART_MAX_ZOOM` in `bases.ts`), enough for the z6 pin
  names to fit but no deeper. The tile pyramid itself is unchanged.
* **"Illustrative base map" chip** on the map (bottom-left on desktop, under the search card on phones) opens a short note: this is a
  stand-in picture, not the in-game geography, and it will be replaced by Rockstar's official map when that is published.
  The legacy base shows "Placeholder map". The text lives in `BaseMap.note`.
* **Fly-to** now aims at the middle of the map left free by the floating controls, so selecting a pin no longer parks it under the
  desktop filter card.

### Labels
Everything baked into the picture that was garbled (MT. KALISIA NATIONAL PARK, GNNGSRIVEYS, LEONIGA KEYS, the Port Gellhorn label,
VICE CITY (MIAMI), VICE BEACH, WATSON BAY, tiny district text, grid numbers, the fake scale bar and both Grok watermarks) was
inpainted out. Correct names are drawn by the site as the six **region pins** (Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn,
Ambrosia, Mount Kalaga), styled like the rest of the site. On the legacy base those chips sit below its baked labels; on the art base
(`bakedLabels: false`) they are the labels.

## Coordinate system (read this before touching pins)

* **Stored pin coordinates never change**: `pins.x/y` live in the canonical *pin space* (`COORD_SPACE`, 1168 x 784, origin top left).
  The Supabase data and the `submit_location` RPC are unchanged.
* Each base maps pin space to its own image pixels (`src/lib/map/bases.ts`):
  * `legacy`: the linear calibration in `base-map.json`.
  * `art`: a **piecewise-affine (Delaunay) transform** fitted through control points (`scripts/art/control-points.json`: region anchors,
    VC and Keys landmarks, plus frame corners), generated into `src/lib/map/pin-transform-art.json` (44 triangles). It is exactly
    invertible (`src/lib/map/pin-transform.ts`).
  * Known pins (all 22 official and 42 GTADB seed pins) also have a hand-checked **position on the art**
    (`src/lib/map/art-pin-positions.json`, from the transform, adjusted by `pinOverrides` and snapped to land with
    `assets/map/art/landmask.png`). Pins without an entry use the transform.
* **Clicking/dragging on the map** converts art pixels back to pin space with the inverse transform before saving, so a community
  submission is stored consistently and shows in the same place on the art base, and at the matching place on the legacy base.
  Round-trip error is under 0.1 pin-space units.
* `?coords=1` shows pin-space coordinates under the cursor for either base.
* `?base=art|legacy` selects the base (default art).

### Accuracy caveats
The art does not follow the old map's geography exactly, so positions come from judgement-based control points, not surveying.
Every seed pin was already `approximate: true` and stays so. Expect place pins to be off by roughly the size of a district,
and re-check them against the official map when it is released.

## Swapping to Rockstar's official map
Add a base in `bases.ts` (manifest from `npm run map:tiles -- <img> --id <name>`, a pin transform, attribution), make it
`DEFAULT_BASE`, and keep or drop the others. See also section 6 of `docs/COMMUNITY_MAP.md`.
